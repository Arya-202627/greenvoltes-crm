PROJECT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT  
Custom Solar EPC CRM & ERP Platform for Greenvolt Energy Solutions LLP  
Project Overview  
We are a solar EPC company engaged in rooftop solar installations under PM Surya 
Ghar Yojana and MNRE subsidy programs. We require a web -based CRM and ERP 
platform that can manage the complete business workflow from lead generation to 
subsidy processing, l oan management, installation, inventory, accounts, and after -
sales service.  
The platform should support multiple user roles including Admin, Dealer, 
Employees, Installation Teams, Loan Executives, MNRE Executives, and 
Customers.  
The system should be scalable to support future expansion across Kerala and 
India.
